## 1. Information We Collect

We collect your personal data only as needed to provide the TukDaeng watch marketplace service, as follows.

**Information you give us directly**
- Account information: email, display name, profile picture, and authentication details (password, or the Apple/Google account used to sign in — with Sign in with Apple we may receive a private relay email address)
- Content you create: listings, posts, comments, images, and chat messages between users
- Personal collection data: purchase prices, purchase dates, and the sale history of your watches (this information is private and visible only to you)
- Contact details when you reach out to our support team

**Information we collect automatically**
- Usage data: searches, saved items, settings, and in-app activity (only what is needed to provide the service — analytics and personalization data is collected only with your consent)
- Device and session data: device type, operating system, and session information to keep your account secure

## 2. How We Use Information

We use your information to:

- **Provide the core service** (necessary for the contract): create and manage your account, display listings, power search, enable buyer–seller chat, send Watch Alert notifications, and manage your collection
- **Keep the service and system secure** (legitimate interest): detect abnormal usage, maintain sessions, handle reports and content moderation, and retain audit logs of administrator actions
- **Meet legal obligations**: keep consent records, process account deletion requests, and comply with applicable laws
- **Analytics and personalization** (only with your consent): analyze usage to improve the app and show content that matches your interests — you can turn this off anytime at Settings > Privacy & cookies

**We do not track your activity across other companies' apps or websites, and we do not share your information with advertisers or data brokers.**

We may share only the information needed with service providers that help us operate the app (Apple/Google authentication providers, push notification services, image storage services, and email providers), some of which have servers located overseas — we send only the data necessary for their work and require them to protect your information to the standard required by law.

## 3. Data Protection & Retention

- We keep your information for as long as your account remains active, and apply security measures such as connection encryption, access-rights restrictions, and masking sensitive data from staff who do not need it.
- When you delete your account, it is suspended immediately and enters a **30-day** grace period (you can restore it by signing in again) — once the period ends, the system removes your identity from data that must be retained under law, such as audit records and report evidence.
- Certain types of data required by law or needed to defend against disputes are kept in de-identified form for defined periods — for example, chat history content is deleted within 1 year, report records are kept for 2 years, and security audit logs for no more than 2 years (see the retention schedule in `pdpa-data-processing-register.md` §3 — DEC-53).

## 4. Your Rights

Under the Personal Data Protection Act B.E. 2562 (2019), you have the right to:

- Access and request a copy of your personal data
- Rectify inaccurate data (you can edit it yourself in your profile)
- Delete or destroy your data — use the Delete Account menu in Settings or contact us
- Withdraw consent for analytics and personalization anytime at Settings > Privacy & cookies
- Object to or restrict processing, and lodge a complaint with the supervisory authority (Office of the Personal Data Protection Committee — PDPC)

You can exercise your rights through the contact channels listed in Help & Support (privacy requests are handled per `pdpa-dsr-sop.md` — DEC-54) — we will respond within 30 days.

When we change this policy, we will notify you and ask for your renewed consent before you continue using the service.
